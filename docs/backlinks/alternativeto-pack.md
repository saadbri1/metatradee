## Description

> MetaTradee is a web-based trading journal and performance-analytics platform
> for forex, futures and metals traders.
>
> It recomputes P&L, R multiple and risk-reward server-side from entry, exit,
> quantity and fees, using one engine for both imported and manually logged
> trades — so the figures in the journal, the analytics and an exported report
> reconcile rather than drifting apart. Money is stored in exact-numeric types
> rather than floating point.
>
> Trades are imported from CSV or JSON statements exported from your platform.
> Ready-made column mappings exist for MetaTrader 4 and 5, cTrader, DXtrade,
> Match-Trader and TradeLocker, with a generic mapping for other formats. Import
> runs as a dry-run preview with content-hash de-duplication before anything is
> written. There is no automatic broker sync and no credential storage.
>
> Other shipped modules: a timezone-correct performance calendar by day, session
> and hour; versioned playbooks that become immutable once used, with adherence
> measured at trade time; psychology tracking with a transparent discipline
> score; bar-by-bar replay of recorded sessions; and composable reports.
>
> An optional AI coach reviews your logged trades and cites the specific trades
> behind each observation. It does not issue trading signals, predict prices or
> give financial advice.
>
> Backtesting — manual or automated — is not available.

## Key capabilities (factual list)
